Brightline Systems - Employee Leave Policy
Version 2.0, effective 1 July 2026
Sample document for a fictional company. It contains no real employee data.

This policy explains the types of leave available to all full-time employees of
Brightline Systems. Part-time employees receive leave in proportion to their
contracted hours.

1. Annual leave
Every full-time employee is entitled to 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar
year. Annual leave builds up at 1.67 days for each full month worked. Requests
must be submitted in the HR portal at least 10 working days in advance and need
approval from the employee's line manager. Up to 5 unused days can be carried
over into the next year; carried-over days must be used by 31 March or they are
lost. Unused annual leave is not paid out in cash, except when employment ends.

2. Sick leave
Employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Sick leave does
not carry over. An employee who is ill must tell their line manager before 10:00
on the first day of absence. A medical certificate is required for any absence
longer than 3 consecutive working days.

3. Parental leave
The primary caregiver of a new child is entitled to 16 weeks of paid parental
leave. The secondary caregiver is entitled to 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Parental leave must start within 12 months of the birth or adoption, and the
employee should give HR at least 8 weeks' notice.

4. Public holidays
Brightline Systems observes 10 public holidays each year. The list is published
in the HR portal every December. Employees who are asked to work on a public
holiday receive a replacement day off within the following 30 days.

5. Remote work days
Employees may work remotely for up to 2 days per week, agreed in advance with
their line manager. Remote work days are not a type of leave and do not reduce
annual leave. The office security pass must be returned when an employee leaves
the company.

6. Questions
Questions about this policy should be sent to hr@brightline.example.
