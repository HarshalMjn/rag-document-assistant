Acme Corp IT Security Policy

Passwords and sign-in
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and must not reuse any of the last 5 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for email, the HR portal and the VPN. Authenticator apps are preferred over SMS codes.
Passwords are never shared, not even with the IT helpdesk. IT staff will never ask for your password.

Devices
Company laptops are encrypted with full-disk encryption and must lock the screen automatically after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Personal USB storage drives may not be connected to company laptops.
A lost or stolen laptop or phone must be reported to the IT helpdesk within 1 hour, by calling extension 4357.

Networks
The company VPN must be used whenever you work from a public network, such as a cafe, hotel or airport Wi-Fi.
Guests use the separate "Acme-Guest" Wi-Fi network and never the internal network.

Phishing and incidents
Suspicious emails must be reported with the "Report phishing" button in the mail client, or forwarded to security@acme.example within 24 hours.
If you clicked a suspicious link or entered your password on an unknown site, change your password immediately and call the IT helpdesk.

Software
Only software from the company software catalogue may be installed. Requests for other software go through the IT helpdesk and need approval from the security team.
Security updates must be installed within 7 days of release.
