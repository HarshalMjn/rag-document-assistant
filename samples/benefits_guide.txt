Acme Corp Benefits Guide

Health insurance
Health insurance covers the employee, a spouse or partner, and up to 2 children. Coverage starts on the first day of employment.
Dental care is reimbursed up to $1,500 per calendar year. Eye care, including glasses or contact lenses, is reimbursed up to $300 every 2 years.

Parental leave
The primary caregiver receives 16 weeks of fully paid parental leave. The secondary caregiver receives 4 weeks of fully paid parental leave.
Parental leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Parental leave is separate from annual leave and does not reduce it.

Learning and development
Every employee has a learning budget of $1,000 per calendar year for courses, books and conferences. Unused learning budget does not carry over.
Courses longer than 3 days need approval from your manager.

Wellbeing
The company reimburses gym or sports club memberships up to $40 per month.
Employees can use the confidential Employee Assistance Programme for free counselling, 24 hours a day.

Referrals
Referring a candidate who is hired earns a referral bonus of $2,000, paid after the new employee completes 90 days.

Retirement
The company matches pension contributions up to 5% of base salary.
